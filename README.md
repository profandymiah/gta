# Inside GTA — Wasted. Busted. Remade.

A resource built by Prof Andy Miah. An interactive insight deck on the history of **Grand Theft Auto**, from *Race'n'Chase* at DMA Design in Dundee (1995) to the launch of *Grand Theft Auto VI* (19 November 2026).

**Live site:** https://profandymiah.github.io/gta/

## What's inside
- 36 slides in 7 chapters, an "In conversation" Q&A,, plus a Watch section and five perspective slides by Prof Andy Miah: Origins · The 3D leap · Moral panic · Prestige to platform · GTA as research instrument · Labour & leaks · Leonida (GTA VI)
- An interactive, filterable timeline of 49 events
- A tile-map atlas of every GTA setting
- A release-gap chart, a claim-vs-evidence toggle on violence research, flip cards on GTA in science, and embedded official trailers
- A gameplay archive with videos from all 16 editions (1997–2026) and a side-by-side comparison player
- Ten insights, research questions and a reading list

Navigate with ← →, swipe, or press **M** for the chapter menu. Each slide has its own link (`#1` … `#33`).

## Notes
A single self-contained `index.html` (no build step). All graphics are original; trailers are embedded from Rockstar Games' official YouTube channel. Independent educational resource, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Launch-week content reflects reporting up to 17 Nov 2026.
